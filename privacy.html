Privacy Policy

The Brazilian Capoeira Academy — San Antonio, TX
Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how The Brazilian Capoeira Academy ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our social media automation application. This application is an internal tool used solely to publish content to our own social media accounts.

2. Information We Collect

This application does not collect personal information from end users. It operates entirely on behalf of The Brazilian Capoeira Academy to automate content posting. The only data handled are:

  • OAuth tokens for our own social media accounts (stored locally, never shared)
  • Video and image files we create and own
  • Public analytics data from our own social media profiles

3. How We Use Information

Authentication tokens are used solely to publish content to our own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and X. We do not sell, share, or transmit any data to third parties beyond what is required by each platform's API.

4. Data Storage

All tokens and credentials are stored locally on our private systems and are never uploaded to public servers or shared with any third party.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Our application connects to third-party platforms via their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they handle data. We are not responsible for third-party data practices.

6. No User Data Collection

This application does not have end users, does not display a user interface to the public, and does not collect, process, or store personal data from any individual other than the business owner operating the tool.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@go-cap.com or visit www.go-cap.com.
