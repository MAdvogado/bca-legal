Privacy Policy

The Brazilian Capoeira Academy — San Antonio, TX
Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how The Brazilian Capoeira Academy ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in two contexts: (1) prospective students and parents who contact us through our website, lead forms, or advertising on platforms like Facebook and Instagram, and (2) our internal social media automation application, which is a private tool used solely to publish content to our own social media accounts.

2. Information We Collect From Prospective Students & Parents

When you submit a lead form (including Facebook/Instagram Lead Ads), contact us through our website, or sign up for a free trial class, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address (if provided)
  • Your child's age or age range (for kids' program inquiries)

3. How We Use This Information

We use this information solely to contact you about scheduling a free trial class, answer questions about our programs, and follow up regarding enrollment. We do not sell this information to any third party.

Your contact information is stored in our customer relationship management (CRM) system, GoHighLevel, and may be used to send you SMS text messages and calls related to your inquiry. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS communication, or contact us directly at the information below to request your information be removed.

4. Information We Collect Through Our Social Media Automation Tool

Separately, we operate an internal, private application used only to publish content to our own social media accounts. This tool does not have public end users and does not collect personal information from the general public. The only data it handles is:

  • OAuth tokens for our own social media accounts (stored locally, never shared)
  • Video and image files we create and own
  • Public analytics data from our own social media profiles

Authentication tokens are used solely to publish content to our own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and X, and are stored locally on our private systems.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Our lead forms and automation tool connect to third-party platforms (including Meta/Facebook, GoHighLevel, and Google) via their official APIs. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how they independently handle data. We are not responsible for third-party data practices beyond our own use of the information described above.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain lead and customer contact information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry or provide our services, or until you request deletion. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the information below.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@go-cap.com or visit www.go-cap.com.
